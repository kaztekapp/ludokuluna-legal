Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Ludo Mafia: Board Game ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile board game developed by KAZTEK LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, username, and display name. You may also add a profile photo and a short bio.

Profile Photo: If you choose a photo from your library or take one with your camera, it is uploaded to our storage and shown to other players as your avatar. We only access your photos or camera when you choose to set a photo.

Messages and Other Content: We store the direct messages, group chat messages, and in-game chat you send, and the names you give to groups and tournaments, so they can be delivered and shown to the people you play with. Offensive words are filtered automatically.

Reports: If you report a message, a player, or a group, we store the report, the reason you chose, and a copy of the reported content so we can review it.

Game Data: We collect game statistics such as wins, losses, streaks, levels, achievements, coins, friends, and whether you are online.

Device Information: We collect your device's push notification token (to send you notifications you allow) and basic technical details such as device model, operating system version, and app version.

Crash Reports: When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report (the error, device model, operating system and app version) to help us fix it. Crash reports do not include your name, email, or messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the game experience
  • To manage your account and profile
  • To enable multiplayer features, friends, chat, groups, tournaments, and matchmaking
  • To display leaderboards and friend lists
  • To keep the community safe: filtering offensive words and reviewing reports
  • To show advertisements (for non-VIP users)
  • To process subscriptions
  • To send push notifications (with your permission)
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Sign-in, database, and storage of profile photos
  • Google AdMob: Advertising (for non-VIP users)
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management and purchase validation
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: App distribution and billing
  • Sentry: Crash and error reports
  • Expo: App updates and push notifications
  • Fly.io: Hosting of our online game server

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy governing the use of your information.

4. Advertising

We display ads through Google AdMob to non-VIP users. The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. On iPhone, ads therefore do not use your device's advertising identifier; on Android, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings. AdMob may also use information such as your approximate location (from your IP address) and the app you are using to choose ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy choices. VIP subscribers enjoy an ad-free experience.

5. Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment information. Purchase receipts are validated through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier linked to your account.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

7. Children's Privacy

Ludo Mafia: Board Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Update or correct your information through the app
  • Delete your account and associated data at any time, in the app under Settings > Account > Delete account
  • Opt out of personalized advertising

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, your personal data is removed immediately; copies in routine backups expire within 30 days. Purchase records may be kept by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat as required for billing and tax. See our account deletion page for details.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app.
