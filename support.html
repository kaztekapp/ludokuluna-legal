Common Questions

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the link we email you.

How do I manage or cancel VIP?
VIP is billed by Apple or Google. On iPhone, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android, open the Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open the VIP screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I report or block someone?
Use the Report option on a message, a player, or a group. Reported content is hidden for you straight away, and you can block the player. Read our Terms of Service for the rules.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, open Settings > Account > Delete account. See the account deletion page for what is deleted.
