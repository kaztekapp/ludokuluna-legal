Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. This page explains how to delete your Ludo Mafia: Board Game account and what happens to your data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Ludo Mafia: Board Game app on your device
  2. Open Settings (the Settings tab at the bottom of the screen)
  3. In the Account section, tap Delete account
  4. Read what will be deleted, then confirm twice

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by contacting us directly at support@mkaztek.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and the email address associated with your account.

What Data Is Deleted

Data Type Action
Account credentials (email, password) Deleted
Profile (username, display name, avatar, bio) Deleted
Game statistics (wins, losses, streaks, coins) Deleted
Friends list and friend requests Deleted
Direct messages and group chat messages Deleted (a direct conversation is removed for both people)
Achievements and leaderboard entries Deleted
Groups you created Handed over to another member (deleted if nobody else is in the group)
Tournament entries Removed (a tournament you created that has not started is cancelled and the other players are refunded)
Your seat in finished games with other players Kept without your name, shown as "Former player", so other players' game history stays correct
Reports about abusive content Reports you sent are kept without your name; reports about your account are kept for safety reviews
Purchase records Kept by Apple, Google and our subscription provider (RevenueCat) as needed for billing and tax

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your game progress, coins, achievements, and social connections are permanently removed as soon as you confirm.

When It Happens

Deleting your account in the app takes effect immediately: you are signed out and your account can no longer be used. Any copies left in our routine backups expire within 30 days. If you ask us by email instead, we delete your account within 30 days of your request.

Active Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel active subscriptions. Please cancel your subscription before deleting your account to avoid future charges: on iPhone, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions; on Android, open the Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
